Privacy Policy

For users in Italy and the EU

This website may collect the data you submit through the contact form: name, email address, subject, and message.

This data is used only to respond to your inquiry, discuss music production, licensing, collaborations, or customer support.

Contact form messages and newsletter sign-ups are stored in this website's database hosted by Cloudflare. Contact messages are forwarded to the site owner by email through Resend; if the website's own system is unavailable, the form is processed by a Google Apps Script endpoint instead. Newsletter emails are kept until you unsubscribe; contact messages are kept as long as needed to handle your request.

To count listens without duplicates, the website stores an anonymous code made from a one-way hash of your IP address and browser, which changes every day and is deleted after two days. Your IP address itself is never stored.

External platforms linked on this website may include Instagram, X, BeatStars, and email services.

Your saved beats and the last beat you listened to are stored only in your own browser (local storage) so the player can remember them on your next visit. This data never leaves your device and you can remove it by clearing your browser's site data.

If you are in Italy or the EU, you may have rights under GDPR, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and the right to contact the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.
